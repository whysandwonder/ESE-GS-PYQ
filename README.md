# UPSC ESE Prelims Paper I PYQ Portal (2017–2026)

Every question from UPSC Engineering Services (Preliminary) Paper I, General Studies and Engineering Aptitude, 2017 to 2026: 1,000 questions with full answer analysis.

- Practice subject-wise or paper-wise, or take random timed tests
- Every option and statement explained
- **Analysis**: how often each broad subtopic is asked, with rising and falling trends and recent years
- Bookmarks, progress tracking and night mode; works on phones
- Progress is saved in your browser; use **My data** to back it up or restore it

## Answer keys

- **2017–2024**: UPSC's official final answer keys. Each question was also solved independently; where our working disagrees, the question is marked "In doubt" with an explanation.
- **2025–2026**: UPSC has not published official keys yet. Answers were solved independently and checked against a published unofficial key; where we believe that key is wrong, our verified answer is shown and the difference is explained.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step or server is needed.

## Publish with GitHub Pages

1. Create a new repository and upload everything in this folder (keep the `data` and `img` folders).
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**, set **Source** to *Deploy from a branch*, choose `main` and `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.
3. After a minute or two the site is live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repository-name>/`.
